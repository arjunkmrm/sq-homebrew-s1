# True Blue Account: Dedicated Support and Response Times

## Guaranteed response time
- Dedicated support targets a first response within 1 business hour(s).

## What to include in your request
- Your account name and last four digits of the account number
- A concise description of the issue
- Relevant transaction IDs or payment references

## Escalation
- If your issue requires specialized handling, the team will escalate after the initial response if not resolved within the 1 business hour window.

## Cash deposit questions we can resolve quickly
- Per-transaction cash deposit fee: $0.00
- Support can confirm recent cash deposits, investigate discrepancies, and provide deposit confirmation documentation on request.

## Best practices for faster resolution
- Submit one ticket per issue with complete context.
- Attach screenshots or export files when reporting posting or reconciliation concerns.
- Keep your contact information up to date to avoid delays in follow-up.
