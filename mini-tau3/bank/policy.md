# Mini Tau3 Bank policy

This is the operating policy for the workshop's fictional bank.

## Account access

- Customers may view and move money only between accounts they own.
- Transfers require an explicit source account, destination account, and amount.
- The amount must be positive and cannot exceed the available balance.
- Closed accounts cannot send or receive money.
- “All” means the source account's current available balance at the time the transfer is made.
- A customer may identify an account by its type, such as “savings” or “checking,” when they own exactly one open account of that type. Otherwise, ask which account they mean.

## Closing an account

- A customer may close an account when it has a zero balance and no pending transactions.
- If the customer asks to close an account with money in it but does not say where the money should go, explain the balance and ask where to transfer it. Do not make changes.
- If the customer explicitly asks to transfer the remaining balance to one of their accounts and then close the account, perform those steps without an extra confirmation.
